About IPEC

IPEC is one of the leading engineering colleges in Uttar Pradesh, affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU). The college is known for its academic excellence and state-of-the-art infrastructure. Founded in 2000, IPEC has consistently produced skilled engineers and professionals.

"Empowering minds, creating futures." – IPEC Motto

The college emphasizes both STEM education and personality development.

Departments at IPEC

  • Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
  • Information Technology (IT)
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
  • Mechanical Engineering (ME)
  • Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
